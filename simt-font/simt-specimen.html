Simtسمت

Simt Font is a Latin and Arabic typeface about direction. Every stroke enters on one compass bearing, which rises to the right in Latin and to the left in Arabic, so both scripts lean toward the way they are read.

Three rules carry the whole design

nmh بنت

Bearing wedges

Stems and teeth start with a cut at one fixed angle. Latin cuts rise to the right and Arabic cuts rise to the left.

dbE

Bearing notches

Where a bowl or an arm leaves a stem, a narrow diagonal notch opens the join. This keeps heavy weights clear and gives the type its signature.

ي ن ر

Horizon finals

Arabic finals sweep low and thin out to a fine tip, like a line running to the horizon.

One letter, four shapes

Arabic letters change shape depending on their position in a word. Simt draws each form, and your browser chooses the right one as you type.

Le simt est la direction que suit le voyageur vers son but, comme la boussole qui indique toujours le nord. Une écriture qui garde son cap reste lisible, même lorsque la route est longue.
