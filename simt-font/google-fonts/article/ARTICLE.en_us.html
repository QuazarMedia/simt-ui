Simt Font is a Latin and Arabic typeface themed around direction, compasses and space. Simt (سمت) means azimuth: the bearing a traveller holds.

Bearing wedges, ink-trap notches and low horizon finals give the two scripts a shared rhythm. The family includes Regular and Bold weights, Arabic shaping and vowel placement, Persian letters, lam-alef ligatures and Latin kerning.
